# Evidence and score reference

TRIDENT has one-second windows at 0.25-second hops. Halmstad has nonoverlapping one-second windows. A video score appears when its window finishes. The 5 to 6 second score appears at 6 seconds. Recording timing is nominal, not measured inference latency.

RFVS has one saved observation per example. Its 30 ms duration belongs to RF, rather than to the camera still or model runtime. The RF display shows the last of four consecutive 7.5 ms spectrogram chunks generated with the released adapter from the original IQ capture. The chart shows saved points without a fabricated score history.

TRIDENT videos reconstruct all 300 original JPEGs at nominal 30 fps. Their WAVs and rendered RF images are original files. The RF pane uses the last frame actually included in the selected cached window. Halmstad excerpts retain the first 300 original frames from each source recording, covering the ten released windows. They do not use unpaired audio.

Pipeline routes illustrate the architecture and selected inputs. They are not measured attention or an explanation generated by Qwen. Qwen normalizes the fixed yes/no answer scores. Fusion classifier uses classifier probabilities. Neither output is calibrated confidence.

TRIDENT labels inherit recording-level presence and its released test reuses recordings observed in Version 1. RFVS labels come from exact-stem label files. Halmstad labels come from recording classes. No frame-level visibility annotation is implied.
