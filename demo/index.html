01 / ENCODE

Three sources become tokens.

Qwen vision encodes the camera frames. The trained RF CNN reads spectrograms. CLAP encodes audio.

TRIDENT example window · 8 camera frames / 4 RF frames / 1 audio crop

02 / FUSE

Queries read the available evidence.

Each stream is projected to width 512. Sixteen learned queries combine the tokens through two attention blocks. Offline streams are zeroed and masked out.

Sensor switches remove whole streams.

03 / CLASSIFY

Fusion can decide directly.

The Fusion classifier pools the fused tokens and scores drone presence. A score of at least 0.5 gives a drone decision.

The first model card follows this branch.

04 / CONNECT

Qwen receives sensor evidence.

The connector maps the 16 fused tokens into Qwen's 4,096-dimensional embeddings. Native camera frames also enter Qwen when camera evidence is enabled.

Frozen Qwen detector scores the fixed answers yes and no.

This is an architecture explanation. The browser selects real saved outputs. It does not run these models or display measured attention.
